02 · Who

Who I am

I build WordPress plugins and the backend systems behind them. The work is usually the unglamorous kind: a payment flow that has to survive an interrupted tab, an internal review tool where one role must not see another's records, a booking table where sensitive fields must not sit in plain text.

I am comfortable owning a requirement end to end, which includes deciding what not to build. Most of what I write is invisible on the page, and that is the point.

I also maintain RankKernel, an independent open source SEO and schema engine. It is my own work, separate from anything done as part of employment.

04 · Technical Focus

Technical focus

Four areas where the majority of the effort goes.

  • Plugin architecture. Extending WordPress through hooks and its extension points rather than replacing templates, so an update does not undo the work.
  • Backend and data. REST endpoints, schema design for custom tables, form entry metadata and server side query scoping.
  • Integration. Signed REST communication, webhook receivers, asynchronous polling for slow services and credential isolation behind the PHP backend.
  • Security. HMAC SHA 256 payload signing, nonce verification, capability gated access and authenticated encryption with key material held outside the database.
