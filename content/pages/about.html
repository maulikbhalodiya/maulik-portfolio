01 · Who I Am

Engineering custom WordPress and PHP systems

I am a WordPress Developer and PHP Engineer focused on custom software functionality rather than visual page assembly. My work centers on what happens beneath the surface of an application: how custom plugins structure their PHP classes, how sensitive records are encrypted before database storage, how distributed environments verify HMAC SHA 256 signatures and how external APIs communicate asynchronously.

I treat WordPress as an extensible application platform that benefits from disciplined software engineering: Composer autoloading, modular responsibilities, strict capability verification and maintainable data models.
