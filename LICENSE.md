## Third party components

This theme bundles no third party JavaScript, no jQuery, and no third party fonts. All
web fonts are self-hosted. There are no outbound HTTP requests of any kind at runtime,
so no third party receives a log of visitors.

Build-time dependencies listed in `composer.json` and `package.json` are not shipped
with the theme. They are development tools only, and each remains under its own licence.

`assets/styles/generated/_theme-json-tokens.scss` is generated by
`scripts/generate-scss-tokens.mjs` and is covered by this same licence.
